Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Motivation For Years Clips ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish short gameplay highlight videos to the owner's own accounts.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel, check the processing and copyright status of those uploads, and delete them if they are blocked. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube: an OAuth access token for the owner's channel, plus the IDs, privacy and upload/rejection status of videos the app itself uploaded.
  • Instagram and TikTok: an access token, the account ID and username, and the IDs of posts the app published.
  • Twitch: public clip information (title, view count, streamer name) from the Twitch API.

The app does not access, collect or store information about any other YouTube users, viewers or channels.

How the data is used, stored and shared

Tokens and post IDs are stored only on the owner's own computer, in local files. The app has no server, no database and no analytics, and it does not use cookies. Data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos. It is never sold or shared with anyone; videos are sent only to the platform they are being posted to.

Retention and deletion

Stored post IDs are kept for up to 14 days for status checks, and rendered video files are deleted after 14 days. Tokens are kept until access is revoked. Deleting the app's local folder removes all stored data immediately.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page. Instagram and TikTok access can be revoked in each platform's connected-apps settings.
