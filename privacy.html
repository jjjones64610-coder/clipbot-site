Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Motivation For Years Clips ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What the app accesses

When the owner authorizes the app with TikTok, Instagram or YouTube, it receives an access token and basic profile information (such as account ID and display name) for the owner's own account. It uses these only to upload and publish the owner's videos.

Storage

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database, and it does not collect data about other people.

Sharing

The app does not sell or share any data. Videos are sent only to the platform they are being posted to.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings, which ends the app's access to that account.
