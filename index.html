Motivation For Years Clips

A small personal tool that turns trending GTA 6 highlight moments from Twitch streams into short vertical videos and posts them to its owner's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. Every video credits the original streamer.

It is used only by its owner to manage their own accounts. It does not offer sign-ups to the public.

How it works

  1. Finds the most-viewed GTA 6 clips on Twitch from the last 24 hours (public Twitch API).
  2. Scans each clip for nudity on the owner's computer and skips anything flagged.
  3. Renders a vertical video with a credit to the streamer.
  4. Uploads it to the owner's YouTube channel using YouTube API Services, then checks its status. Clips with copyright claims or blocks are deleted everywhere.

This app uses YouTube API Services. Users agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
